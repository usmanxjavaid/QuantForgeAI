# QuantForgeAI

QuantForgeAI is an AI-powered financial research and paper-trading platform designed to combine deterministic trading systems with agentic AI.

## Vision

The platform is being developed as a research and engineering system for:

- Market data collection and normalization
- Technical and quantitative analysis
- Strategy development
- Backtesting
- Risk management
- Portfolio simulation
- Paper trading
- AI-assisted market research
- Multi-agent decision workflows
- Performance evaluation

The project initially focuses on cryptocurrency markets, with architecture designed to support additional markets such as forex and equities later.

## Current Status

Early development.

The current milestone is building the market-data foundation that will support research, strategies, backtesting, risk management, and paper trading.

## Disclaimer

QuantForgeAI is a research and engineering project. It does not claim profitable trading performance and is initially designed for paper trading and simulation rather than real-money trading.
